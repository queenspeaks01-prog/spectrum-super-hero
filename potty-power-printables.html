💙 Caregiver's Gentle Guide

  • Offer short, pressure-free bathroom opportunities at predictable times, such as after meals.
  • Use a stable footstool and let the child choose whether to sit.
  • Model simple words or picture cards: “Change please,” “Potty,” and “Help.”
  • Check diapers or pull-ups regularly and change promptly after stooling.
  • If stool smearing happens, respond calmly. Wash hands and skin with soap and water and disinfect affected surfaces safely.
  • Notice patterns: time, stool consistency, discomfort, activity, and what happened before the behavior.
  • Talk with a pediatrician about recurrent smearing, constipation, leakage, pain, or skin irritation. Severe pain or vomiting requires prompt care.
