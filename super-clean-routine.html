🛁 My Bath-Time Picture Routine

For younger children — point to each picture and do one step at a time.

🧸1. Pick my towel or toy
🌡️2. Grown-up checks water
🦶3. Get in with help
🧼4. Wash gently
🚿5. Rinse
🧻6. Dry and get dressed

My words matter: “Too loud.” “Too hot.” “Help please.” “Break please.”

It's okay to start with a washcloth, a few steps, or a shorter routine.

⭐ My Independent Hygiene Checklist

For older children — check off the steps you complete, with help when needed.

  • Gather towel, washcloth, clean clothes, and soap
  • Check that the water feels comfortable with caregiver guidance
  • Wash face and body gently
  • Wash hair when it is hair-wash day
  • Rinse away soap and shampoo
  • Dry off and put on clean clothes
  • Put used clothes and towel in the right place

My comfort plan: I can ask for a break, quieter water, a different washcloth, or help.

💙 Caregiver's Gentle Bathing Guide

Bathing resistance can be connected to sensory discomfort, transitions, communication, anxiety, fatigue, or painful skin conditions. Try curiosity before correction.

  • Offer choices: shower or bath, washcloth texture, fragrance-free products, and the order of steps.
  • Use a predictable picture routine and a gentle countdown before transitions.
  • Reduce sensory stress: lower water pressure, use a cup to rinse, dim bright lights, and avoid splashing the face.
  • Keep the bathroom comfortably warm. An adult should test the water temperature and supervise younger children around water at all times.
  • Respect privacy and body autonomy. Explain each step and ask before helping with washing when possible.
  • Celebrate participation rather than demanding a perfect bath. A shorter wash can be a successful start.
  • Ask a pediatrician about persistent pain, severe skin irritation, or sudden changes in bathing tolerance; occupational therapy may help with sensory routines.

Safety: Never leave a young child alone in a bathtub or around standing water, even briefly. Keep soaps and cleaning products out of reach.
